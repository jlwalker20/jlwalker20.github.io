Addons for WoW: Forever

Jaff's Better Forever UI

A dark, quiet interface for the Forsaken in all of us: small addons that fix, darken and tidy the default UI, built for keyboard and Steam Deck alike.

Full package

Everything: panel + all addons

Every addon on this page plus Jaff's Better Forever UI Beta, the in-game options panel that gives each installed addon a tab, so you can change settings without editing files.

  • Open the panel with /jaffs (or the Jaffs UI macro), or from the minimap's addon button.
  • Every tab has an on/off switch for that addon, and a Reload button for settings that need one.
  • Gamepad controls for the panel are still to come; use the mouse for now.

Installing a zip: on the Steam Deck, open Downloads in Dolphin, right-click the zip, Extract → Extract archive here, then drag the folders into _forever_/Interface/AddOns/ and replace when asked. On a PC, right-click, Extract All, and drag the same way. Then /reload or restart the game.

Look

A full dark-grey reskin of the default UI, plus reshaped cast bars and a square minimap.

DarkIronUI

v1.19

Recolors the default UI from gold and brown to a consistent dark grey: unit frames, gamepad and keyboard action bars, XP bar, bag bar, menu bar, cast bars, minimap, quest tracker and tooltips. Icons, bar colors and quality colors stay as they are. Every part can be toggled on its own. Replaces FlatUnitFrames and GamepadDarkMode; delete those first.

CastBarPlus

v0.9

Makes your cast bar taller and puts the spell name inside the bar, centered, with the cast timer on its right edge. The border keeps its proportions instead of stretching.

TargetCastBarPlus

v0.11

The same look for your target's and focus's cast bars: taller, with the spell name and a cast timer inside the bar and the spell icon centered beside it. The bars sit above their frames, drawn in front of the buff icons; switch each one back below with the TCB Target and TCB Focus macros.

SquareMinimap

v0.10

Makes the minimap square with a border built from Blizzard's own frame pieces (Diamond metal, Dialog edge and more), tinted to match DarkIronUI. Keeps the north arrow on the top edge and lines the zoom buttons up under the bottom-right.

Quality of life

Small fixes for chat, party and raid frames, travel, gathering and mana.

ShortChannels

v1.5

Shortens chat channel names: General becomes G, Trade becomes T, Trade (Services) becomes TS, Trade (Local) becomes TL, and so on.

Download README

ChatFade

v1.3

Fades the chat window after 30 seconds without a new message and brings it back when one arrives, on zone changes, or when you open the input box.

Download README

TargetClassColors

v0.4

When you target another player, the target frame's health bar turns their class color. NPCs keep Blizzard's normal colors.

Download README

PartyFramesImproved

v3.1

Everything for Blizzard's raid-style party and raid frames: party frames stack from the bottom up with you on top and everyone else in class order; raid frames grow from the bottom-right in the same order, with "Keep Groups Together" on or off; levels on names; leader crown and target marker on the health bar; the top-left shows the faction icon (gray until PvP-flagged), the role icon, or nothing (/pfi slot); incoming heal and shield bars that run past the frame's edge. Replaces PartyGrowUp, PartyLevels and DispelGlow. Commands: /pfi help.

A small movable readout of your MP5 straight from the game, and a countdown for the five-second rule after you spend mana. Wand shots, fishing, mounting and zone effects don't reset it. Movable in Edit Mode. Commands: /mr.

Priest

WeakenedSoulTracker

v0.9

For priests: a cooldown-style icon for everyone you shield, with their portrait, class-colored name and a countdown until Weakened Soul ends. Works in combat (it tracks your casts, not auras), and Renewed Hope heals take time off. Movable in Edit Mode, three saved spots for solo, party and raid. Commands: /ws help.
