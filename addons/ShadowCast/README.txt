ShadowCast 0.2 (test)
=====================
Priests only. While you're in Shadowform, a dark blue-purple layer covers the
healing spells on your action bars (Lesser Heal, Heal, Greater Heal, Flash
Heal, Renew, Prayer of Healing, Binding Heal, Circle of Healing, Prayer of
Mending, Penance, Desperate Prayer, Holy Nova, Lightwell, Resurrection).
Leave the form and it goes away.

It's a layer of its own on each button; the buttons themselves aren't
changed.

INSTALL
  Copy the ShadowCast folder into:
    World of Warcraft/_forever_/Interface/AddOns/

COMMANDS
  /sc           status: whether it sees Shadowform, how many buttons it covered
  /sc alpha 55  layer strength, 10-100

SETTINGS
  In /jaffs with Jaff's Better Forever UI: layer strength.

Built for WoW: Forever, client 1.60.1 (live build 70790).
