Power rankings

League notes

The standings and the rankings now agree at the top. Kevanescence, End Around Chris, Monday Night Jason and Game Plan Jemma are the top four here and the top four in Yahoo's standings, in the same order.

One lineup call decided a game. The Lion Ass Bitches left Jake Ferguson's 18.30 on the bench behind T.J. Hockenson's 4.40. That swap alone gets to 111.54 against 110.60. The other four losing teams would have lost even with their best lineup.

The middle of the table is a pile-up. Fifth through eighth are separated by 3.2 points, less than a single kicker week. Week 3 should sort some of it out.

Quarterbacks are dropping. Caleb Williams, Jayden Daniels and Jaxson Dart all got hurt in Week 2, and all three are on rosters in this league.

Week 3 matchups

Projections are Yahoo's own. Weekly scores swing roughly 22 points either side, so anything inside a six-point margin is close to a coin flip.

Injury report

Jayden Daniels, QB, Washington — dislocated elbow. Out Week 3, likely longer. Daniels left the loss to Dallas with an elbow dislocation; early X-rays showed no fracture. Washington is starting Marcus Mariota in Week 3, multiple Mariota starts are reported as likely, and an IR stint hasn't been decided. For the Lion Ass Bitches, C.J. Stroud is already on the roster and outscored Daniels last week, 17.02 to 14.74.

Caleb Williams, QB, Chicago — hamstring strain. Week to week. Williams pulled his hamstring against Minnesota and didn't return. An MRI showed a strain with no tear. Reports say he could play in Week 3 but will likely miss some time. Monday Night Jason has Bo Nix behind him, who scored 15.12 on the bench.

Nico Collins, WR, Houston — hamstring. About two games. A grade 1 strain suffered in practice before Week 2. Houston's expectation was that he misses two games, and Week 2 was the first. The Bitches have no receiver on the bench, so the flex has to cover.

Puka Nacua, WR, LA Rams — hip. No timeline. Nacua was inactive for Monday night and scored 0.00 from Filthy Rich's lineup. There's no reported timeline for his return, and Filthy Rich has one receiver on the bench.

Brock Bowers, TE, Las Vegas — knee. Chance for Week 3. Bowers was inactive for Week 2, his second straight missed game after the meniscus trim. The latest reporting gives him a chance to return in Week 3. Michael Mayer has the Raiders' tight end work until then.

A.J. Brown, WR, New England — high-ankle sprain, on IR. Around Week 7. No change. The six-week estimate from Week 1 points to a return around Week 7 or 8.

Zay Flowers, WR, Baltimore — hamstring. Out Week 2. Flowers missed Week 2 and scored 0.00 on End Around Chris's bench. His Week 3 practice reports will tell you how long this runs.

Jaxson Dart, QB, NY Giants — knee. To be determined. Dart left Monday night's game against the Rams. Early reporting says he probably sprained his MCL, and the Giants' GM said he'll be OK. He's on End Around Chris's bench behind Lamar Jackson, so it barely matters here.

Also on the report. Saquon Barkley left with a stinger, returned in the second half, and is expected to play Monday. Mike Evans left with a hip injury, reportedly as a precaution, and is said to be fine. Malik Nabers hurt his shoulder Monday night and returned to the game. D.J. Moore is day to day with an AC joint strain after one touch. Dallas Goedert hurt his knee but has an extra day with a Monday night game. Rico Dowdle was pulled twice with a toe injury, which means more work for Jaylen Warren. Jadarian Price left with a chest injury, but X-rays were negative. J.K. Dobbins has a hamstring injury, which opens a role for rookie Jonah Coleman. Jayden Reed was carted off Sunday and has no timetable.

Power score = average weekly starter points + 35% of average bench points + 8 × (wins − losses) / games played, minus an injury adjustment.
The injury adjustment is the projected weekly points lost against the best replacement already on the roster, doubled for absences of four weeks or more. Movement is measured against the Week 2 rankings.
