League notes

The tight end cliff decided more than anything else. Bowers, McBride, Loveland and Warren all went inside the first 33 picks. The gap between owning one of those four and not is the largest single positional edge in the league.

Discipline against ADP tracked with quality. The three teams that gained the most ground versus average draft position all finished in the top five here; the team that gave up the most finished last.

Treat the gaps as small. Six of the ten teams project within five points a week of each other. Over a fourteen-game season that is roughly a one-game spread, which is well inside the noise of a fantasy season.
