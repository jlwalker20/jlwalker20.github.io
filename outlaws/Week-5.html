Week 5 power rankings v1

Blended standing for the ten-team half-PPR league after four weeks: average starter points, bench counted at a third, an adjustment for the record, and an injury adjustment for what each roster is missing going into Week 5.

Posted after Week 4 · before Week 5 kickoff

Power rankings

League notes

End Around Chris is 4-0 and did it in the least likely way: Kyler Murray started over a questionable Lamar Jackson, Jackson scored 18.88 on the bench, and Chris won by twenty anyway. Nobody else in the league has fewer than two losses.

One swap cost Eagle Eyed the game. Devaughn Vele scored 14.40 on the bench while Stefon Diggs scored 6.00 in the flex. That one change makes it 126.34 and beats Filthy Rich's 124.42. Game Plan Jemma's best lineup still falls 2.40 short, and the other two losing teams weren't close.

Byes start now. Kansas City and Carolina are off in Week 5, which is most of the reason eight of the ten projections sit under 105 and the defending power leaders are projected in the eighties.

The season low moved. 72.48 is the fewest points any roster has scored this year, under Monday Night Jason's 78.72 in Week 2 and Kevanescence's 78.94 in Week 3.

Week 5 matchups

Projections are Yahoo's own. Weekly scores swing roughly 22 points either side, so anything inside a six-point margin is close to a coin flip. Kansas City and Carolina are on bye.

Injury report

Saquon Barkley, RB, Philadelphia — hamstring. Week to week. Barkley left on the Eagles' second drive against the Rams and finished with 1.50. Reporting says it isn't considered serious and he avoided long-term damage, but it could still keep him out of Week 5 in London. Tank Bigsby took 14 carries in his place.

Ladd McConkey, WR, LA Chargers — foot. Uncertain. McConkey was active, then ruled out during the third quarter with an aggravated foot injury, scoring 0.00 from a bench slot. This is the second time the foot has flared up in three weeks.

Ja'Marr Chase, WR, Cincinnati — concussion. Likely out Week 5. Chase left in the second quarter after a facemask and entered the protocol immediately; he's widely expected to miss the Miami game. Tee Higgins went for 11 catches and 157 yards without him, then picked up an adductor injury of his own and is day to day.

Lamar Jackson, QB, Baltimore — ankle. Day to day. Jackson hurt the ankle on a second-half sack and didn't return. Baltimore hopes to have him for Sunday night against Atlanta, but head coach comments left Week 5 open. Chris has Kyler Murray as cover and already started him once.

Rashee Rice, WR, Kansas City — hamstring. Possibly several weeks. Rice left in the first quarter and scored 0.00 in End Zone Christina's lineup. Kansas City is on bye in Week 5, so he may not technically miss a game, but reports suggest the absence could run longer.

DeVonta Smith, WR, Philadelphia — hamstring. Multiple games. Ruled out for Week 4 and expected to miss more than one game. He scored 0.00 in Kevanescence's lineup.

Caleb Williams, QB, Chicago — hamstring. Out Week 5. Williams won't play in Week 5 and hasn't practiced, still week to week on the three-to-four-week timeline from the Week 2 injury. Bo Nix has the job on Monday Night Jason, and Bryce Young outscored him 21.46 to 13.56 last week.

Justin Jefferson, WR, Minnesota — ankle. Hopeful for Week 5. Jefferson could return against New Orleans with final medical clearance. He has missed one game.

Jayden Daniels, QB, Washington — elbow. Could play Week 5. Daniels is working toward a Week 5 return against the Giants, though Washington may be down both him and Mariota. C.J. Stroud scored 23.08 for the Bitches in the meantime, so there's no rush.

Also on the report. Breece Hall is still week to week with the quad. Terry McLaurin missed Week 4 with a hamstring and scored 0.00. A.J. Brown remains on IR until around Week 7. Travis Etienne is on IR. Jonathan Taylor is off the injury report entirely after the Week 4 scare.

Power score = average weekly starter points + 35% of average bench points + 8 × (wins − losses) / games played, minus an injury adjustment.
The injury adjustment is the projected weekly points lost against the best replacement already on the roster, weighted by how long the absence is expected to run. Bye weeks are not counted. Movement is measured against the Week 4 rankings.
