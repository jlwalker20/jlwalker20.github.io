Power rankings

League notes

Week 3 cost more than any week so far. De'Von Achane tore his ACL and is out for the season, Justin Jefferson has a high ankle sprain that's being read as about four weeks, Breece Hall is week to week with a quad, and Travis Etienne aggravated a hamstring. That's on top of Nacua, Daniels, Caleb Williams and A.J. Brown. Seven of the ten rosters are missing a starter.

Three teams lost games their bench would have won. Peasant Lewis lost by 0.92 with Matthew Stafford's 22.90 behind Josh Allen's 18.96. The Lion Ass Bitches left Bowers (22.60) and McLaurin (16.70) out of a 131.84–113.58 loss. Game Plan Jemma benched Jakobi Meyers (15.90) and Cam Skattebo (11.50) in a six-point loss.

End Around Chris is alone at 3-0 and has scored 104.90 or better every week without ever topping 151. Nobody else in the league has been under 20 points of variance week to week.

Third and fourth are 0.03 apart. Kevanescence scored 78.94 after two weeks over 129, and Eagle Eyed's 146.90 was the highest score anyone has posted since Week 1.

Week 4 matchups

Projections are Yahoo's own. Weekly scores swing roughly 22 points either side, so anything inside a six-point margin is close to a coin flip.

Injury report

De'Von Achane, RB, Miami — torn ACL. Out for the season. A non-contact knee injury in the Week 3 loss, confirmed by Schefter as an ACL tear. Achane scored 1.70 in End Zone Christina's lineup before leaving. Ollie Gordon is the first waiver call in Miami, with Jaylen Wright behind him. This is the single most expensive injury in the league so far.

Justin Jefferson, WR, Minnesota — ankle. About four weeks. His foot was trapped under a defender and the read is a high ankle sprain, with projections pointing to a Week 8 return. High ankle sprains cost receivers about four weeks on average and tend to dull production after that. Jordan Addison is the beneficiary in Minnesota, and Filthy Rich loses its top receiver a week after getting nothing from Nacua.

Breece Hall, RB, NY Jets — quad. Week to week. An MRI came back better than feared, but an absence is still expected and Braelon Allen takes the backfield. Hall scored 7.00 for Filthy Rich before the injury.

Travis Etienne Jr., RB, New Orleans — hamstring. Expected to miss time. He aggravated the hamstring and is expected out, with Alvin Kamara and Kendre Miller splitting the work. Christina loses a second starting back in the same week.

Caleb Williams, QB, Chicago — hamstring. Back around Week 5 or 6. Williams missed Week 3. Schefter reported a grade 2 strain with a three-to-four-week timeline from the Week 2 injury, which makes Week 4 unlikely. Bo Nix scored 25.14 on Monday Night Jason's bench while Bryce Young started.

Puka Nacua, WR, LA Rams — hip. Likely back Week 4. Doubtful and inactive for Week 3, but reporting says he should play against Philadelphia barring a setback. Surgery has been mentioned only if he doesn't improve.

Jayden Daniels, QB, Washington — dislocated elbow. Back within three weeks. Daniels avoided surgery and an IR stint, and is expected back within three weeks of the Week 2 injury, which puts Week 4 or 5 in play. C.J. Stroud has the job on the Lion Ass Bitches until then.

Nico Collins, WR, Houston — hamstring. Decent chance for Week 4. Collins has now missed two games. Reporting gives him a decent chance to return this week.

A.J. Brown, WR, New England — high-ankle sprain, on IR. Around Week 7. No change from the six-week estimate.

Also on the report. Brock Bowers is back and scored 22.60 from a bench. Zay Flowers returned and scored 12.90, also from a bench. Dallas Goedert sat out Week 3 with the knee. Jayden Reed still has no timetable. Jonathon Brooks is on IR. Baker Mayfield hurt his thumb, which matters for Tampa's pass catchers.

Power score = average weekly starter points + 35% of average bench points + 8 × (wins − losses) / games played, minus an injury adjustment.
The injury adjustment is the projected weekly points lost against the best replacement already on the roster, weighted by how long the absence is expected to run. Movement is measured against the Week 3 rankings.
