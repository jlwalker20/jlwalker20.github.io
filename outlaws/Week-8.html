Power rankings

League notes

Five teams are 3-4. Monday Night Jason, CeeDee Sam, Peasant Lewis, End Zone Christina and the Cliiimb are all tied, separated only by points, and they are fighting over the last two playoff places. Two teams are 5-2 and effectively through; everyone else is in the scrum.

End Around Chris streamed the defense and scored 142.70, the highest total anyone has posted since Week 1. He also left Tony Pollard's 35.00 on his bench, which is the kind of problem you want.

Three teams lost games their benches would have won. Monday Night Jason benched Jameson Williams (23.60) and Josh Jacobs (17.60) and lost by 24. Filthy Rich started Purdy for 14.02 with Burrow's 28.88 sitting there. Christina benched Drake Maye for 28.44 and won anyway, which is now three straight weeks of the same decision.

Four rosters had an unset slot on Wednesday. Peasant Lewis had Josh Allen and James Cook on the bench behind Stafford and a bye-week starter, Kevanescence still had the 49ers defense in with San Francisco off, and Ari and Christina had empty slots. Projections here score the best lineup each roster could field, so those four are counted at full strength rather than as they stood.

Week 8 is the heaviest bye week of the year. Houston, New Orleans, the Giants and San Francisco are all off, and it hits unevenly: Peasant Lewis gets Josh Allen and James Cook back and becomes the week's biggest riser, while CeeDee Sam loses four players at once.

Week 8 matchups

Our projection: 45% of a team's season starter average, 55% of its last three weeks, then adjusted for the best lineup each manager can field — bye players benched, replacements slotted in from the bench, a streamed kicker or defense where the roster has none. Lineups are read as they stood after Wednesday's waivers, and each team below names what the projection counted in place of a bye, an injury or an unset slot. These are scoring assumptions, not advice — nobody's lineup is being second-guessed here. Matchup notes in the write-ups come from published defense-versus-position data and are context only — they are not an input to the projection, because across nine starters the favorable and unfavorable spots largely cancel. Houston, New Orleans, the Giants and San Francisco are on bye. Weekly scores swing roughly 22 points either side, so anything inside a six-point margin is close to a coin flip.

Playoff picture

Six of ten make it

The regular season runs through Week 13. The top six seeds reach the championship bracket, the top two get a first-round bye, and the quarterfinals start in Week 14. Seeding is by record, with total points as the tiebreaker — which is the only thing separating five teams at 3-4.

Odds below come from simulating the remaining six weeks 40,000 times. Each team scores from its own distribution each week — its blended scoring level, less the injury adjustment, with the same 22-point weekly swing used everywhere else on this page — and the simulated standings are seeded by the league's own rules.

Injury report

Christian McCaffrey, RB, San Francisco — hamstring. Back in Week 9. He left Week 7 after scoring 19.00 with what Shanahan called a mild strain, and San Francisco is on bye in Week 8, so he misses nothing extra. The team is optimistic about Week 9 against the Raiders. Kaelon Black is the handcuff worth holding.

Saquon Barkley, RB, Philadelphia — back. Uncertain. He returned from the hamstring, took seven carries for 62 yards and a touchdown, then left with a back injury and did not return. Philadelphia is expected to be cautious. He scored 14.10 from a bench slot.

Emeka Egbuka, WR, Tampa Bay — ruled out. No timeline. Ruled out after failing to progress through Tampa Bay's medical protocols, and he scored 0.00 from a starting spot in Week 6 before being benched.

Tyler Warren, TE, Indianapolis — groin. No timeline. Ruled out again after the groin flared up in late-week practice, with further testing planned. That is three straight weeks without him.

Lamar Jackson, QB, Baltimore — ankle. Expected back Week 8. He missed Week 7 and was limited in practice, but is expected to return against Buffalo. That is a straight upgrade on Kyler Murray for End Around Chris.

Breece Hall, RB, NY Jets — quad. Week 8 is realistic. A fourth straight missed game in Week 7, but Week 8 is described as a realistic target for his return. He is on Filthy Rich's bench.

Aaron Jones, RB, Minnesota — knee. Day to day. He aggravated the knee in Week 7 and did not return. Schefter reports it is not considered significant but he may miss Week 8 against Detroit, with Jordan Mason starting if he sits.

Mike Evans, WR, San Francisco — Achilles. Week to week, uncertain. Still sidelined and still described as serious. He has not played since Week 6 and is on IR.

Cam Skattebo, RB, NY Giants — hamstring. Expected to be fine. He felt it late in an overtime win after 17 carries for 124 yards and a touchdown. Garafolo says he should be okay, and the Giants are on bye in Week 8 regardless.

Also on the report. Rashee Rice has been ruled out again with the hamstring. D.J. Moore is expected back for Week 8 after the Buffalo bye. Zach Charbonnet's return appears imminent. Jeremiyah Love is out with an ankle sprain. Jalen Hurts, Terry McLaurin and De'Von Achane remain out for the season, and Davante Adams is still six to 10 weeks away.

Power score = average weekly starter points + 35% of average bench points + 8 × (wins − losses) / games played, minus an injury adjustment.
The injury adjustment is the projected weekly points lost against the best replacement already on the roster, weighted by how long the absence is expected to run. Bye weeks are not counted in it. Week 8 projections and playoff odds are our own, not Yahoo's, and assume each manager fields the best lineup available from their own roster. Movement is measured against the Week 7 rankings.
