Blended standings for the ten-team half-PPR league after one week: Week 1 starter points, bench counted at a third, a fixed swing for the result, and an injury adjustment for what each roster is missing going forward.

Posted after Week 1 · before Week 2 kickoff

Power rankings

League notes

Benches decided more than lineups. Roughly 190 points sat unused across the league. Several losing teams had a better lineup available, but only Game Plan Jemma would have flipped the result with it.

One week is not a verdict. Weekly scores swing about 22 points either side of a team's true level, so a power gap under fifteen points is inside the noise. The bottom five are separated by eleven.

The injury column is the only part that carries forward. Week 1 points are already banked. A.J. Brown's ankle, Kyler Murray's concussion and Zay Flowers' hamstring are what change lineups in October.

Week 2 matchups

Projections are Yahoo's own. Weekly scores swing roughly 22 points either side, so anything inside a six-point margin is close to a coin flip. Four of these five are inside four.

Injury report

A.J. Brown, WR, New England — high-ankle sprain, on IR. About six weeks. Brown had his ankle rolled up on in the third quarter at Seattle and finished with 4.10. An MRI confirmed a high-ankle sprain with no fracture, and NFL Network reported he is expected to miss roughly six weeks with no surgery planned. New England placed him on injured reserve, so the minimum is four games regardless. Mack Hollins, Romeo Doubs and DeMario Douglas absorb the targets; none is worth chasing.

Kyler Murray, QB, Minnesota — concussion. Out Week 2. Murray took a late, penalized helmet shot from Javon Bullard eleven snaps into his Vikings debut and did not return. Both Rapoport and Schefter have indicated he misses Week 2 against Chicago. Carson Wentz came in and threw three touchdowns. Concussion timelines are individual and cleared by independent doctors, so this could be one week or three — and Jayden Daniels is already on that roster.

Brock Bowers, TE, Las Vegas — meniscus trim. Likely back. Bowers had the procedure on Sept. 8 and missed Week 1. Schefter reported the Raiders see a realistic chance he returns in Week 2 at the Chargers. It is the same knee that cost him time in 2025, so the timeline is worth watching, but the short version is that he is probably available.

Ladd McConkey, WR, LA Chargers — chest and ribs. Week to week. Left in the third quarter against Arizona after going 5-82-1 and still finished as the Colonoscopy Co.'s second-best starter at 16.70. Reporting suggests a rib injury being described as a chest injury — painful, usually not more than a game or two.

Zay Flowers, WR, Baltimore — hamstring. No timeline. Flowers put up 23.50 and appears to have re-aggravated a previous hamstring issue. Rashod Bateman is the Baltimore beneficiary, more so with Ja'Kobi Lane out several weeks with a wrist fracture.

Sam Darnold, QB, Seattle — hip and glute. Week to week. Went down five plays into the opener and finished with 0.52. Drew Lock took over and threw for 187 and a touchdown. Darnold was QB2 depth to begin with and can be dropped without much thought.

TreVeyon Henderson, RB, New England — ankle. No timeline. Still has not practiced since Aug. 24 and has not played. Kevanescence has him on IR, which is the right place for him until New England says something concrete.

Around the league. Tua Tagovailoa (oblique) is out in Atlanta with Cooper Rush starting, which caps Drake London and dampens the environment around Bijan Robinson. De'Zhaun Stribling is out at least a month with a deltoid ligament injury, concentrating targets around Mike Evans in San Francisco. Dylan Sampson exited Cleveland's loss, so Quinshon Judkins' workload is worth monitoring.

Power score = Week 1 starter points + 35% of bench points, ±8 for the result, minus an injury adjustment.
The injury adjustment is the projected weekly points lost against the best replacement already on the roster, doubled for absences of four weeks or more. Movement is measured against the post-draft rankings.
