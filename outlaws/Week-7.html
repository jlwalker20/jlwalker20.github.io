Power rankings

League notes

Four teams are 4-2 and three are 3-3. Seven of the ten are within two games of each other, and the gap between the fourth seed and the seventh is 68 points of scoring across six weeks.

The closest game of the week could not have gone the other way. Jordan beat End Around Chris 110.94 to 108.82, and Chris's best available lineup — Pollard in for Irving, the only upgrade on his bench — finishes on 110.52. He loses by 0.42 with a perfect Sunday.

Filthy Rich posted 61.86, the lowest score anyone has managed this season, with Gordon, Burrow and Jefferson all on bye. It is 4-2 anyway.

These projections assume best lineups. Three rosters had an empty slot or a bye player starting on Wednesday, and End Around Chris has no defense on his bench at all. We credit each manager with the best lineup available to them, which is what turns the matchup of the week from a Filthy Rich edge into a half-point coin flip.

Three season-enders in one week. Jalen Hurts has blood clots and is done, Terry McLaurin tore a patellar tendon, and Blake Grupe tore up a knee on his Rams debut — a quarterback, a starting receiver and a kicker, on three different rosters.

Week 7 matchups

Our projection: 45% of a team's season starter average, 55% of its last three weeks, then adjusted for the best lineup each manager can actually field. We assume everyone fills an empty slot from their own bench, benches anyone on bye, starts the better of two quarterbacks, and streams a defense or kicker if the roster has none — so these are best-case lineups, not Wednesday's. The assumed move is named on each team below. Buffalo, Jacksonville, the Chargers and Washington are on bye. Weekly scores swing roughly 22 points either side, so anything inside a six-point margin is close to a coin flip.

Playoff picture

Six of ten make it

The regular season runs through Week 13. The top six seeds reach the championship bracket, the top two get a first-round bye, and the quarterfinals start in Week 14. Seeding is by record, with total points as the tiebreaker — which is how the four 4-2 teams and the three 3-3 teams are currently separated.

Odds below come from simulating the remaining seven weeks 40,000 times. Each team scores from its own distribution each week — its blended scoring level, less the injury adjustment, with the same 22-point weekly swing used everywhere else on this page — and the simulated standings are seeded by the league's own rules.

Injury report

Jalen Hurts, QB, Philadelphia — blood clots. Out for the season. What started as a superficial calf clot is now deep vein thrombosis with bilateral pulmonary emboli. He is on blood thinners for roughly three months and cannot do football activity in that time; the Eagles expect a full recovery but he is done for 2026. Tanner McKee starts in Philadelphia. CeeDee Sam has moved to Kirk Cousins, with Tyler Shough behind him.

Terry McLaurin, WR, Washington — torn patellar tendon. Out for the season. He played through it for 3.20 in Week 6 and the diagnosis followed. Ari loses a starting receiver for good, though Washington's Week 7 bye softens the immediate blow.

Davante Adams, WR, LA Rams — ruptured hamstring. Six to 10 weeks. McVay put the range at six to 10 weeks, which covers most or all of what is left of the fantasy regular season. Jemma has him on IR. Nacua is the beneficiary in Los Angeles.

Blake Grupe, K, LA Rams — knee. Out for the season. Hurt early in his Rams debut after Peasant Lewis picked him up to cover the Bates bye. Bates is back from Detroit's bye, so the kicker spot is covered, but Lewis is out an emergency option.

Lamar Jackson, QB, Baltimore — ankle. Day to day. He has missed two games. Baltimore is targeting a Week 7 return against Cincinnati if he gets through a full week of practice. He is on Chris's bench behind Kyler Murray.

Mike Evans, WR, San Francisco — ribs and Achilles. At least two weeks. Ruled out in Week 6. Reporting says the Achilles is not torn but he will miss time, and San Francisco has a Week 8 bye behind this one. He is on Jordan's bench.

A.J. Brown, WR, New England — back from IR. Playing. Brown returned in Week 6 for 4.20 after missing five games and is in Jordan's starting lineup for Week 7.

Saquon Barkley, RB, Philadelphia — hamstring. Nearing a return. He has progressed to straight-line running and still needs change-of-direction work, but he is back in Jordan's lineup this week.

Tyler Warren, TE, Indianapolis — groin. Possible Week 7 return. Downgraded and then ruled out for Week 6, which made Freiermuth the right start. Groin injuries linger, so watch the Colts report.

Also on the report. Quinshon Judkins missed Week 6 with the ankle and his Week 7 status is unclear. Breece Hall has been activated off IR to Filthy Rich's bench. Justin Jefferson is healthy, activated and starting. Ladd McConkey is in concussion protocol with the Chargers on bye. Deebo Samuel, Rashee Rice and Kyle Monangai are all still out. Jacory Croskey-Merritt is on IR for at least four games and Romeo Doubs has been placed on IR. D.J. Moore has a shoulder injury with Buffalo on bye.

Power score = average weekly starter points + 35% of average bench points + 8 × (wins − losses) / games played, minus an injury adjustment.
The injury adjustment is the projected weekly points lost against the best replacement already on the roster, weighted by how long the absence is expected to run. Bye weeks are not counted in it. Week 7 projections and playoff odds are our own, not Yahoo's, and assume each manager fields the best lineup available from their own roster rather than the one set on Wednesday.
