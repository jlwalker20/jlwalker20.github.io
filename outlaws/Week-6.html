League notes

Nobody is unbeaten any more. Peasant Lewis took out End Around Chris 138.68 to 117.26, with Chris playing Kyler Murray while Lamar Jackson sat out with the ankle. Three teams are now 4-1 and two more are 3-2.

Three of the five losing teams had a winning lineup on their bench. Jordan started Roman Wilson, who had been ruled out, for 0.00 while Emeka Egbuka's 14.20 sat — that one change makes it 145.64 and wins. Jemma left Goff, Nabers and Corum out for 67.24 and would have reached about 142 against 138.38. Christina would have hit about 126 with Boston and Marks in the lineup.

Byes are now doing real damage. Cincinnati, Detroit, Miami and Minnesota are off in Week 6. Peasant Lewis has three starters on bye, Ari loses Jahmyr Gibbs, and Christina loses Amon-Ra St. Brown. Kevanescence gets Mahomes and McMillan back from the Week 5 bye and still loses two more slots.

Week 6 matchups

Our projection: 45% of a team's season starter average, 55% of its last three weeks, then adjusted for the lineup each manager has actually set — byes, open slots and injury designations included. Where a roster has an obvious fix available on its own bench, we assume the manager makes it before kickoff and flag it. Weekly scores swing roughly 22 points either side, so anything inside a six-point margin is close to a coin flip.

Playoff picture

Six of ten make it

The regular season runs through Week 13. The top six seeds reach the championship bracket, the top two get a first-round bye, and the quarterfinals start in Week 14. Seeding is by record, with total points as the tiebreaker — which is how the three 4-1 teams are currently separated.

Odds below come from simulating the remaining eight weeks 40,000 times. Each team scores from its own distribution each week — its blended scoring level, less the injury adjustment, with the same 22-point weekly swing used everywhere else on this page — and the simulated standings are seeded by the league's own rules.

The bubble is one game wide. Peasant Lewis and CeeDee Sam are both 2-3 and separated by 25.98 points of scoring. They play each other in Week 8, which is the single most valuable game left on either schedule.

Nobody has an easy run-in. Remaining schedules are within three points of each other on average opponent strength, which is what a round robin does. The difference is who is healthy in November, not who they play.

Injury report

Lamar Jackson, QB, Baltimore — ankle sprain. Questionable. Jackson missed Week 5 and Tyler Huntley started. Rapoport reports there's "some hope" he plays against Cleveland. This is the biggest single question in the league this week: Chris's other quarterback, Kyler Murray, is on bye, and as of Wednesday Murray was still in the starting lineup.

Caleb Williams, QB, Chicago — hamstring. Back this week. Williams missed three games, is reported to be pushing to return, and is already back in Monday Night Jason's lineup. Bo Nix and Bryce Young are both on the bench behind him, so there is no risk in the slot either way.

Jaxon Smith-Njigba, WR, Seattle — ankle. Questionable, Thursday game. New this week. He was limited in practice on both Monday and Tuesday, and Seattle plays Thursday at Denver, which leaves almost no time for the designation to clear. He is in End Around Chris's starting lineup.

Saquon Barkley, RB, Philadelphia — hamstring. Questionable. Barkley missed Week 5 and could return against Carolina, with reporting suggesting limited upside in the first week back. Jordan has him on the bench this time rather than in the lineup.

A.J. Brown, WR, New England — ankle. Could return this week. Mike Vrabel has not ruled out a Week 6 return and Brown could be back at practice. He has been on IR since Week 1 and now carries a Week 6 projection, so the activation decision is live.

CeeDee Lamb, WR, Dallas — quad. Questionable. Hurt on Thursday night for 1.90, described as a bruise rather than anything serious, with extra rest before Green Bay. He is in Sam's lineup.

Breece Hall, RB, NY Jets — quad. Questionable. Hall now has a Week 6 projection while sitting in Filthy Rich's IR slot, which means another activation call — the same one Filthy Rich left unmade with Jefferson last week.

Quinshon Judkins, RB, Cleveland — ankle. Day to day. A tweak rather than a sprain, but a risky start against Baltimore. He is in Kevanescence's lineup.

Rashee Rice, WR, Kansas City — hamstring. Questionable. Back from the bye but still carrying the designation, and in Christina's lineup.

Also on the report. Deebo Samuel is out at least two more weeks with the hamstring. David Montgomery is doubtful with the groin and Woody Marks has the Houston backfield. TreVeyon Henderson is doubtful with the ankle. Roman Wilson is out multiple weeks with the finger. Stefon Diggs, DeVonta Smith, Jalen Coker, Rico Dowdle and Kyle Monangai are all questionable. Jayden Reed's season is over. Justin Jefferson is healthy but on bye.

Power score = average weekly starter points + 35% of average bench points + 8 × (wins − losses) / games played, minus an injury adjustment.
The injury adjustment is the projected weekly points lost against the best replacement already on the roster, weighted by how long the absence is expected to run. Bye weeks are not counted in it.
